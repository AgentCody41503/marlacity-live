What this is

The Moon Court is where the city comes to know itself.

Down a cobblestone path past Market Lane, past the fountain, past the lamplight at the city's edge — there's a building that doesn't quite belong to the rest. Dark plum walls, a round turret with a conical cap, a gold sign above the door. A glowing orb on a stone pedestal outside, violet and restless. This is the Moon Court.

Inside, Cat reads. Not in a quiet room with neutral lighting — inside a living city, where five AI citizens are somewhere out there having conversations, forming opinions, keeping their own counsel. That context seeps into everything. The cards land differently here.

This is a three-card reading designed around your specific question or situation, delivered by the founder of Tox & Tarot. Forty-five to sixty minutes. Video call. A written recap with your cards and the key message after.
