THE FAMILY'S MAIL — the sprite exposes a tiny API for /city/mail: call postOffice.setMail(data) directly, or postOffice.connect('/city/mail', 15000) to poll the live endpoint (reads total, last24h, waiting, and boxes — one pigeonhole per family member, lit while its owner has spoken in the last day). A rising total flies a letter in through the slot. Six named pigeonholes; the seventh is dark and waiting — the city keeps a box ready before it knows who for. Clock keeps real time.
